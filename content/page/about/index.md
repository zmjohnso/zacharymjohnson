## The Journey

I kicked off my professional life in early 2021 at Atomic Object, a consultancy where I got to wear many hats. During my time there, I jumped between projects—moving from React/Node ecosystems to Angular/.NET Core stacks—and shared some of my learnings over on the [Atomic Spin blog](https://spin.atomicobject.com/author/zach-johnson/).

In May 2025, I made the move to Capital One. These days, I’m focused on building financial tools using Vue and Python, all powered by AWS. I’m a big fan of modernizing legacy systems and making the 'complex' feel simple for the end user.

## Interests & Philosophy

On the technical side, I’m deep into the world of web development, cloud architecture, and the intersection of technology and finance. I also spend a lot of time thinking about Bitcoin and Austrian economics—topics you might see pop up in my writing here as I expand this blog beyond just code.

When I’m not in front of a terminal, you’ll likely find me outside running or practicing balance sports. I find that a good run is often the best way to debug a stubborn problem.
