## Project Themes

### Financial tools and internal applications

At Capital One, I work on financial tools using Vue, Python, and AWS. The common thread is turning complex business and technical requirements into interfaces and services that are easier for teams to trust, operate, and evolve.

### Product consulting across web stacks

At Atomic Object, I worked across React, Node, Angular, and .NET Core projects. Consulting work taught me to move between problem domains quickly, communicate tradeoffs clearly, and leave teams with software they can continue to maintain after launch.

### Content and CMS modernization

Several of my recent writing topics come from hands-on work with Contentful, Next.js, GraphQL, and migration workflows. I enjoy improving the shape of existing systems: cleaner content models, simpler data fetching, better deployment paths, and less friction for editors and developers.
